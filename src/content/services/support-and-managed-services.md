## Support & Managed Services

Salesforce doesn't stay static — your business changes, Salesforce releases updates three times a year, and integrations need maintenance. Our managed services keep your org healthy and your team unblocked, month after month.

---

### Defined SLAs

Every ticket gets triaged and responded to within a defined window:

| Priority | Criteria | Initial Response |
|---|---|---|
| P1 — Critical | Production down, data loss risk | < 1 hour |
| P2 — High | Key process broken, workaround exists | < 4 hours |
| P3 — Medium | Non-blocking bug or enhancement | 1 business day |
| P4 — Low | Cosmetic issue, long-term request | 3 business days |

---

### Ongoing Support & Enhancements

Your retainer hours flex across both maintenance and new development:

- **Bug investigation and fixes** — Root cause analysis with a written summary, not just a patch
- **Enhancement development** — New fields, flows, reports, and features scoped and built within the retainer
- **Release testing** — Pre-release sandbox testing against Salesforce's three annual releases
- **User training** — Short written or video guides for changes that affect end-user workflows

---

### Org Health Reviews

A quarterly deep-dive into your org's security, performance, and maintainability:

- **Security audit** — Profile review, permission set audit, sharing rule analysis, field accessibility
- **Automation debt analysis** — Identifying trigger conflicts, redundant workflows, and flow inefficiencies
- **Field & object utilization** — Removing unused fields, picklist cleanup, object consolidation opportunities
- **API & Apex limits** — Governor limit trends, bulk processing review, and async job health
- **Written report** — Findings prioritized by risk/effort with a recommended action plan

---

### Release Management

Deploying changes to production without surprises:

- **Sandbox strategy** — Developer, Developer Pro, Partial, and Full sandbox management
- **Deployment runbooks** — Step-by-step deployment guides with pre/post validation steps
- **Change set review** — Dependency checking and destructive change impact analysis before any push
- **Post-deploy validation** — Smoke test scripts covering critical business processes
