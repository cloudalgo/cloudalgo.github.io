When Salesforce acquired Heroku in 2010, the goal was clear: give developers a frictionless cloud runtime that works natively with Salesforce data. Over a decade later, that vision holds.

## Heroku Connect

Heroku Connect keeps a PostgreSQL database in sync with Salesforce objects bidirectionally. It's the fastest path to building data-heavy apps without writing sync code.

## Salesforce External Services

Register your Heroku-hosted API as an External Service in Salesforce, then call it from Flow or Apex with zero authentication complexity.

## Dyno Scaling

Scale from a free hobby dyno to production-grade Standard-2X dynos in seconds — no infrastructure work required.
