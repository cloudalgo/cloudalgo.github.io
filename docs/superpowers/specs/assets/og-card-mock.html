The card 39 pages share

Every page without artwork of its own unfurls as /og-default.jpg — services, products, case studies, About, Contact, the legal pages, the Journal index, and six Journal entries headed by a drawn plate. Five options below, all at the real 1200×630, in the live press-room palette and the live faces. Each is shown twice: full size, and at the ~460px an unfurl actually renders, which is the only size that decides whether a card works.

What the current card gets wrong

Palette
Black ground and the old display face — it is the retired _monochrome skin. The site has been Paper and Ember since the redesign.
Claim
“92% success rate” appears nowhere on the site. StatsCounter publishes 1 day response, 15+ clients, 70+ projects, 12+ years combined. A number no page can support should not front 39 of them.
Badge
“Salesforce Consulting Partner” — kept off every option below until someone confirms the partner status is current and the badge is licensed for use.
Scope
Salesforce-only, while the card also fronts Heroku, MuleSoft, AWS, four products and five case studies.
